# KC Clean Trade Branding

## Brand Core

- Brand Name: KC Clean Trade
- Platform Context: KC Clean Trade Workforce Platform
- Primary Audience: พนักงานปฏิบัติการ, พนักงานคลังสินค้า, พนักงานทำความสะอาด, หัวหน้างาน

## Brand Colors

- KC Blue: `#003366`
- KC Yellow: `#FFCC00`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Support Slate (for readability only): `#0F172A`, `#475569`, `#E2E8F0`, `#F8FAFC`

## Brand Personality

- Friendly
- Human
- Trustworthy
- Workforce Focused
- Industrial Workforce
- Practical
- Direct

## Not This Brand

Do not make the experience feel like:

- Government Website
- Generic Job Board
- Startup SaaS
- Corporate Enterprise Portal
- LinkedIn
- JobThai
- Job marketplace

## Typography Guidelines

- Use large, highly legible typography first
- Headings should be bold, short, and instantly understandable
- Body text should be concise and written in plain Thai
- Search, salary, location, and action buttons must be readable on mobile without zooming
- Avoid dense text blocks and long corporate paragraphs
- Prefer short imperative labels over marketing sentences

## Visual Tone

- Clean
- Open
- Friendly
- Simple
- Confident
- Workforce-first
- Industrial but welcoming

Use generous whitespace, clear grouping, and obvious next actions.

## Illustration Style

- Use custom illustrations or simple vector scenes
- Show workforce context such as cleaning, warehouse, factory, transport, and on-site operations
- Prefer geometric, friendly, easy-to-read illustrations
- Use KC Blue and KC Yellow accents in the illustration system
- Avoid stock photos
- Avoid glossy corporate office imagery
- Avoid abstract startup-style 3D graphics that disconnect from real workers
